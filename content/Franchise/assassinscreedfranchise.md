# Real-World Locations from Every Assassin's Creed Game

> **Travel through time and history by visiting the real-world locations from the Assassin's Creed franchise.**

## Overview: Assassin's Creed and Historical Tourism

The _Assassin's Creed_ franchise by Ubisoft is renowned for its meticulously recreated historical settings. From Renaissance Italy to Viking-era England, each game offers a virtual tour of real-world locations that gamers can actually visit. This guide showcases the best **Assassin's Creed real-world locations for gaming tourism**.

---

## [Assassin's Creed (2007)](assassinscreed.md) – The Holy Land

**Key Locations:** Jerusalem, Acre, Damascus  
**Modern Country:** [Israel](Israel.md) / [Palestine](Palestine.md) / [Syria](Syria.md)

[Ubisoft](Ubisoft.md)'s first title transports players to the 12th-century Holy Land. These historic cities are pilgrimage and heritage destinations, rich with Crusader-era architecture and spiritual landmarks.

**Must-Visit:**

- Jerusalem's Old City
    
- Citadel of Aleppo (Syria)
    
- Acre Fortress
    

---

## [Assassin's Creed II](Assassinscreed2.md), [Brotherhood](assassinscreedbrotherhood.md), [Revelations](Assassinscreedrevelations.md) (2009–2011) – Renaissance Italy & Constantinople

**Key Locations:** Florence, Venice, Rome, Constantinople (Istanbul)  
**Modern Country:** [Italy](Italy.md), [Turkey](Turkey.md)

These entries define _Assassin's Creed's_ travel appeal. Renaissance Florence and Venice are almost unchanged today, making them perfect for gaming history buffs.

**Must-Visit:**

- Florence Cathedral (Duomo)
    
- Venice's St. Mark's Basilica
    
- Rome's Colosseum
    
- Hagia Sophia in Istanbul
    

_Explore more in [Gaming Destinations: Italy](Italy.md)_

---

## [Assassin's Creed III (2012)](Assassinscreed3.md) – Colonial America

**Key Locations:** Boston, New York, Frontier (Appalachia)  
**Modern Country:** [USA](USA.md)

This installment offers a rare glimpse into Revolutionary War-era America. While much has changed, heritage sites and walking tours preserve the atmosphere.

**Must-Visit:**

- Freedom Trail, Boston
    
- Historic Williamsburg
    
- Appalachian hiking routes
    

---

## [Assassin's Creed IV: Black Flag](Assassinscreed4.md) (2013) – Caribbean

**Key Locations:** Havana, Nassau, Kingston  
**Modern Country:** Cuba, Bahamas, Jamaica

Set during the Golden Age of Piracy, this title features tropical islands and colonial port towns.

**Must-Visit:**

- Old Havana (UNESCO)
    
- Nassau pirate museum
    
- Port Royal, Jamaica
    

---

## [Assassin's Creed Unity (2014)](Assassinscreedunity.md) – Revolutionary Paris

**Key Location:** Paris  
**Modern Country:** [France](france)

Arguably the most faithful digital recreation of Paris ever made. Many players used the game to tour Notre-Dame after the 2019 fire.

**Must-Visit:**

- Notre-Dame Cathedral
    
- Palace of Versailles
    
- Catacombs of Paris
    

_More in [Gaming Destinations: France](france)_

---

## [Assassin's Creed Syndicate (2015)](Assassinscreedsyndicate) – Industrial London

**Key Location:** London  
**Modern Country:** [UK](UK)

Set during the Industrial Revolution, Syndicate includes Big Ben, the Thames, and Victorian alleyways.

**Must-Visit:**

- Westminster Abbey
    
- Tower of London
    
- British Museum
    

---

## [Assassin's Creed Origins (2017)](assassinscreedorigins) – Ancient Egypt

**Key Locations:** Alexandria, Giza, Siwa Oasis  
**Modern Country:** [Egypt](egypt)

A beautifully crafted world that doubles as a living museum.

**Must-Visit:**

- Pyramids of Giza
    
- Great Library site in Alexandria
    
- Temple of Amun at Siwa
    

_See our [Egypt Gaming Travel Guide](egypt)_

## [Assassin's Creed Odyssey (2018)](assassinscreedodyssey) – Ancient Greece

**Key Locations:** Athens, Sparta, Santorini (inspired), Crete  
**Modern Country:** [Greece](greece)

A mythological setting based on real historic locations.

**Must-Visit:**

- Acropolis of Athens
    
- Ruins of Delphi
    
- Palace of Knossos, Crete
    

_Explore [Gaming Destinations: Greece](greece)_

## [Assassin's Creed Valhalla (2020)](Assassinscreedvalhalla) – Viking England & Norway

**Key Locations:** York, London, Lofoten Islands, Stavanger  
**Modern Countries:** England, Norway

Valhalla explores Norse and Anglo-Saxon culture with stunning landscapes.

**Must-Visit:**

- York Minster
    
- Viking Ship Museum (Oslo)
    
- White Cliffs of Dover
